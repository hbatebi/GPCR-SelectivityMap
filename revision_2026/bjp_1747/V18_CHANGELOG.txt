25. NEW ANALYSIS: the within-receptor multiclass test rerun under sequence-identity-cluster
    validation, which is the confound control applied everywhere else in the manuscript. No model was
    refitted; the analysis reuses the frozen out-of-fold predictions already archived in Source Data
    S11, which turn out to cover every representation under receptor-grouped and 50%, 40% and 30%
    identity-cluster validation. The same 34 receptors and 72 units are eligible under all four
    schemes, so the schemes are directly comparable.
    Result: against that run's own receptor-grouped reference of +0.051 (P = 0.009), the margin was
    +0.061 at 50% identity clusters (P = 0.006; receptor-level mean +0.071, 95% CI +0.003 to +0.139,
    positive in 22 of 34 receptors), attenuated to +0.019 at 40% (P = 0.057) and was absent at 30%
    (P = 0.91). Full sequence, interface sequence and the identity-neighbour baseline reproduced the
    permutation value exactly at every threshold, as the degenerate design requires. Stable across
    five permutation seeds.
    The within-receptor test therefore follows the same identity-threshold profile as the archive-wide
    analysis. Added to Results 3.3, to the Discussion as a convergence argument, to the response to
    Reviewer 2 point 1, as SI Table S31, and as Source Data S16. The 40% and 30% rows are reported as
    attenuation and absence, not as supporting results.

26. REPLICATION FRAMING. The second coordinate feature filter is now presented as an independent
    replication of the within-receptor test rather than only as a baseline for the identity-cluster
    rows. Two filters over the same 207 units, the same receptor-grouped folds and the same 34
    receptors and 72 units are both positive on the probability margin at P below 0.01: +0.065
    (P = 0.008; macro ROC AUC 0.722, Table S4) and +0.051 (P = 0.009; macro ROC AUC 0.778, Table S11).
    They differ on accuracy only in headroom: 0.556 against a permutation 97.5th percentile of 0.556
    in the first filter, and 0.597 against 0.472 in the second, so the second filter's accuracy is
    significant at P = 0.001 with room above the null.
    The designated primary model is unchanged. Switching the primary designation to the filter that
    performs better, after seeing the result, would be a selection the reviewers would rightly flag;
    the first filter remains primary and the second is reported as replication.
    Margins from the two filters are NOT interchangeable. Results 3.3, Table S31's note and the
    response to Reviewer 2 point 1 all state that the identity-cluster ladder
    (+0.051 -> +0.061 -> +0.019 -> -0.034) must be read against the second filter's own
    receptor-grouped value of +0.051, never against the +0.065 quoted for the primary model.

27. CORRECTION of an error introduced in v21. v21 described the two within-receptor executions as

    This correction also disposes of the second-round objection that the primary model was never
    taken through identity-cluster validation: it was. The identity-cluster ladder IS the designated
    primary specification under the confound control, not a substitute for it.
28. CONSTRUCT STRATIFICATION of the within-receptor test (new Table S32, new Source Data S17).
    Experimental method, species and ICL2 completeness are constant within every one of the 34
    eligible receptors; ligand type varies within one. Resolution, deposition year, fusion-protein
    annotation and ICL2-associated ligand jointly explain 2.1% of within-receptor margin variance and
    adjusting for them leaves the mean at +0.051. Resolution-matched subset (<= 0.3 A): +0.074, 19
    receptors, P = 0.033. Single-publication endpoints: +0.097, 8 receptors, P = 0.17. Class-pair
    stratification: Gi/o + Gq/11 receptors +0.085 (18 receptors, P = 0.038), which is the stratum
    where stabilisation chemistry is closely matched because Gq/11 complexes are routinely chimeric
    constructs engineered so the antibody fragment used for Gi/o complexes will bind.
    Antibody-fragment and nanobody identity are separate chains, not receptor fusions or ICL2
    ligands, are not curated in the deposited metadata used here, and could not be annotated. Stated
    explicitly; residual construct confounding is not claimed to be excluded.
29. MULTIPLICITY. Benjamini-Hochberg and Bonferroni corrections across the nine representations,

    0.0099; margin P = 0.0094, q = 0.045. First execution: margin P = 0.008, q = 0.072. Reported in
    Results 3.3, the Table S31 note and Source Data S17. The manuscript now states that the study was
