# Scientific scope and claim boundaries

GPCR SelectivityMap profiles static receptor-chain representations and evaluates their transfer under explicit receptor and sequence-homology controls.

## Supported statements

- A representation is associated with a bound transducer class under a specified validation design.
- A representation transfers or fails to transfer across a specified sequence-identity threshold.
- A predefined interface sequence block does or does not outperform matched non-interface controls.
- A static feature block exceeds or fails to exceed missingness, taxonomy, or sequence-similarity baselines.

## Unsupported statements without further evidence

- A score is a validated functional coupling probability.
- A high receptor-grouped AUC demonstrates receptor-intrinsic selectivity.
- Elastic-network susceptibility is a molecular-dynamics trajectory, free-energy barrier, or kinetic rate.
- Coefficient localization proves causal interface chemistry.
- Failure at stringent homology holdout proves biological absence.

## Relationship to dynamic work

The package does not perform time-dependent helix translations, rotations, hinge-axis analysis, state populations, transition kinetics, or trajectory-derived communication. Those questions require molecular-dynamics or experimental time-resolved data.

## Experimental chemistry modules

The interface-complementarity and receptor-compatibility commands are exploratory. Their scores are not calibrated functional probabilities and are not required for the manuscript's central conclusions.
