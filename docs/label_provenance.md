# Label provenance

Bound-complex and functional labels are not interchangeable.

## Bound-complex label

A transducer family physically present in a deposited complex is a direct structural observation of that complex. It is not automatically evidence of the receptor's complete physiological preference.

## Functional label

A receptor-only functional annotation may be based on direct assays, database consensus, manual curation, or homology-supported inference. The recommended table fields are:

- `label_source`
- `label_type`
- `experimental_support`
- `curation_status`
- `homology_inferred`
- `reference`

Do not describe a label as directly measured unless these fields support that statement.
